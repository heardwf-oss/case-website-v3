Practice

CASE Consultants has been a civil and structural engineering practice in Torquay since 1988. In that time we have taken on thousands of jobs for public bodies, contractors and private clients across Devon and the South West.

What we do

Alongside larger schemes for councils, Network Rail and contractors, much of our work is the day-to-day engineering that construction needs: extensions and new openings, propping and piling platforms, foundations, retaining walls, surveys and insurance reports.

We work on design-and-build and traditional contracts. As well as the design, we prepare contract documents and tender packages and administer contracts.
