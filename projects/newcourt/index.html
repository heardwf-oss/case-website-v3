Newcourt Station

Exeter, DevonPlatform structures beside a live railway

Photograph: Geof Sheppard, CC BY-SA 4.0

A new station with a 124 m platform, built beside a railway line that stayed open throughout.

Client
Network Rail, through Dyer & Butler
CASE role
Detailed structural design
Contract
Design and build
Project value
£0.75m
Year
2015

Our design covered the platform, its retaining walls and foundations, and the precast platform beams and planks.

Because the adjacent single line stayed in service, we worked with Dyer & Butler on how to install the foundations with the least disruption to the track.

The works also included drainage, access routes, a shelter, cycle facilities and passenger information.
